08Good to know

Before we
get started.

Clear expectations make better work. Here are the essentials.

What kind of projects do you accept?

We work on websites, apps, UI/UX systems, academic builds, startup concepts, and focused project support.

Can you work with an early-stage idea?

Yes. You can bring a rough concept, a problem statement, or even just a question. We help shape the next useful step.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. Small design or prototype work can move quickly, while full builds are planned in clear milestones before development begins.

Do you provide updates after delivery?

Yes. We can help with handoff, fixes, documentation, and future improvements after the first version is shipped.
